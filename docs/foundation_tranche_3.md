# UNG Foundation Tranche 3

Group 2 of the remaining shared capabilities:

- synchronized replay/forensics with hash-chained records
- deterministic scenario/fault-injection orchestration for SITL/HITL
- invariant, transition-graph, boundary-vector verification helpers
- storage lifecycle/retention/integrity management
- fleet software/config/calibration/connectivity inventory
- predictive maintenance scoring for vibration, battery, thermal, fan, storage, sensor and actuator health

Platform storage backends, distributed replay persistence, hardware fault injectors and advanced prognostic models remain adapters or later specialized implementations.
